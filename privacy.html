Privacy Policy for Law Empire: Sierra Leone

Effective Date: November 2025

Career Tread Ltd. and Tell Somebody Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") operate the Law Empire application and the website lawempire.xyz.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Law Empire, we collect your email address, display name, and gameplay progress (case scores, level, coins, and chamber upgrades) to enable cross-device cloud synchronization.

2. Payments & Transaction Data

All mobile money (Orange Money, Afrimoney, SafulPay) and credit card payments are processed securely by Monime Sierra Leone (pay.monime.io). We do not store financial account numbers or credit card CVVs on our servers.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your passwords are bcrypt-hashed and account tokens are encrypted. Data is used exclusively for account authentication and cloud save progress.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or wish to request data deletion, contact us at support@lawempire.xyz.
